# Chibichi / Setsuko’s Kitchen
**A bilingual storefront backed by practical kitchen operations.**

[Visit Chibichi](https://chibichi.com/) · [Portfolio](../README.md)

Chibichi connects menu browsing and ordering with the staff workflows needed to prepare and fulfill food orders.

## Capabilities

- English and Japanese menu content.
- Product photos, descriptions, availability, and quantities.
- Cart and checkout.
- Payment guidance and order tracking.
- Staff workflows for fulfillment, inventory, scheduling, and administration.

## Typical journey

Browse the menu → choose items → review the cart → checkout → follow payment and pickup instructions.

Menus, ordering windows, and pickup availability change. The screenshot is a dated example, not a current menu or offer.

The engineering emphasis is coordinating customer-facing commerce with inventory, order state, payment records, and staff operations.
