# RZM8
**Your experience. A stronger resume.**

[Visit RZM8](https://rzm8.com/) · [Portfolio](../README.md)

RZM8 helps a person compare their experience with a target job, inspect requirement coverage, and build a reviewed resume draft.

## Capabilities

- Import supported resume documents or paste editable text.
- Compare a resume with a target job description.
- Review matches, evidence, and gaps.
- Add confirmed experience through guided gap filling.
- Review and edit the draft.
- Export Word documents or print / save PDF.
- Explicitly save projects and reopen them later.

## Typical journey

Resume → Target job → Assessment → Fill gaps → Review draft → Export or save.

Suggestions are intended to stay grounded in the user's supplied evidence. Users remain responsible for confirming experience and reviewing the final resume.

Saving a project is distinct from temporary assessment processing. Saved projects and assessment results have separate retention and availability rules; the application displays the applicable policy.

The engineering emphasis is evidence-based AI assistance, recoverable workspaces, document formatting, and preserving user edits across navigation.
