# Studio media: from a photo to a saved result

Reviewed November 4, 2026. [Portfolio](../README.md) · [NTM8](../products/ntm8.md)

Studio offers image effects and a separate photo-to-video action where enabled. Members select owned media, choose an effect or available video duration, review the price and confirm the required rights/consent before submitting. Illustrative preset artwork communicates style; it is not a guaranteed likeness or output preview.

| Stage | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Quote | Review the current service price before starting. |
| Hold | Sparks are reserved while the job is processed; they are not yet a completed charge. |
| Processing | The application can continue work after the dialog closes. An acknowledgement does not mean the result is ready. |
| Saved result | Successful media appears under the original member's ownership and follows the applicable visibility controls. |
| Settlement or release | Completed delivery settles the quoted charge; failed or cancelled work follows its reservation-release rules. An unresolved outcome may require review. |

The wallet separates transaction history from active holds. Repeated submissions and uncertain network outcomes require care to prevent duplicate work. Product controls and operational review govern recovery; closing a browser does not itself cancel a job.

Video choices come from the current service configuration. Longer duration consumes more processing and affects the quoted price; supported duration alone does not establish generation quality, delivery reliability or response time. A playable stored result, correct visibility and correct settlement each need validation.

Availability, supported inputs, prices and moderation policies are defined by the live application. This overview does not publish internal deployment details, guarantee completion time, or certify production payment and generation acceptance.
